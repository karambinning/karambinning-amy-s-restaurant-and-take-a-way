— Chapter One

A neighbourhood restaurant, not a chain.

Amy's sits in a quiet stretch of Wootton Road, the kind of King's Lynn address residents direct visitors to with a small note of pride. Inside, the kitchen turns out Chinese and Thai cooking the way the regulars want it — generous, steady, the same crisp spring rolls and proper red Thai curry you remember from last visit.

It is a small operation. One dining room. A counter for the takeaway crowd. A driver who knows the postcodes. No theatre, no buffet-cliché. Just food cooked for a neighbourhood that has been ordering it for years.

We open in the evening only, every day of the week. Walk in for a table, ring ahead for collection, or call the number for delivery. The address is 26–28 Wootton Road — just past the petrol station if you're driving down from the A148.
